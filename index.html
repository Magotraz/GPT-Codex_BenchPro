THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Clear from the
first conversation.

We confirm the specifics of each engagement with you before making introductions or commitments.

What happens after I share a role?

Our team reviews the brief, follows up on any missing detail and discusses the search and next steps with you.

How do you assess a match?

We consider the required skills and experience alongside project scope, timing and working arrangements. You review and select from the proposed shortlist.

Can I request a specific location or duration?

Yes. Add these requirements to the brief. We will confirm what is feasible for the role and the locations involved.

Who manages the engagement?

Your team directs the project work. BenchPro coordinates the talent engagement, with contracting and payroll support agreed for each assignment.
