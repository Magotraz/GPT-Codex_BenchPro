Workspace essentials

What happens after I post a role?

Your request goes to BenchPro for review. The team follows up to clarify your requirements and discuss next steps.

Which roles can I see?

Client superusers see all roles for their company. Team members see only roles assigned to them. The overview and role counts follow the same access rules.

How do I add a teammate?

A client superuser can open Team access and invite a colleague using an approved company email domain. Contact BenchPro Admin if a domain needs to be approved.

Where is my job description?

Open a role from the Roles page. If a document is attached, you’ll find it in the role details.

Can I reassign a role?

Yes. As a client superuser, open the role details and select Edit next to the owner. Choose a teammate and confirm the assignment.
